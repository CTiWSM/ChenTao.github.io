Tao Chen 陈涛

School of Mechanical Engineering, Zhejiang University · PhD Student

I am a PhD student in Mechanical Engineering at Zhejiang University. I have published seven papers in internationally recognized journals and conference proceedings indexed in SCI or EI. I serve as a reviewer for several journals in AI and related fields classified in Zone 1 of the Chinese Academy of Sciences journal ranking, including Pattern Recognition (PR), Engineering Applications of Artificial Intelligence (EAAI), and Knowledge-Based Systems (KBS).

My research focuses on medical artificial intelligence and robotics, particularly screening assisted by tongue images, multisource feature fusion, mobile robot localization, and autonomous navigation.
